## Immediate gaps to fill

The inventory is intentionally incomplete. Highest-priority additions are:

- Full V.8, V.21, V.22, V.22bis, V.32, V.32bis, V.42, V.42bis, V.90, V.92, V.250, T.30 and T.38 primary documents.
- V.150.0 and detailed V.150.1 amendment/SPRΤ references.
- RFCs for SDP, RTP redundancy and FEC profiles applicable to PCM/VBD.
- Original Rockwell/Conexant/Lucent/Agere/USR/Motorola modem technical references.
- At least two modem/DSP textbooks with legally inspectable pages for timing recovery, carrier recovery, equalization, echo cancellation and trellis coding.
- Asterisk/FreeSWITCH source-level evidence for modem/fax passthrough handling and DSP settings.
- Externally generated WAV/PCM modem traces and reference bitstreams suitable for regression tests.
- Specific Android device/system-image candidates for privileged telephony-audio experiments.
