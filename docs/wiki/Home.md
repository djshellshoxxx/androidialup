# AndroidDialup Wiki

AndroidDialup is an experimental Android-based dial-up modem platform. It presents a modem-style DTE interface (AT commands
and serial-style data) and carries the connection over IP, with voice-band and modem-relay paths as research topics.

**Status:** research-first. Beta 0.1 is specified before implementation. The Android app compiles in CI, but runtime
behaviour on a physical phone is not yet established (README).
